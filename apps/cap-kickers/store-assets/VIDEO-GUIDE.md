# Cap Kickers — gameplay video (record on-device)

Both stores treat the video as **optional**, but it helps players get the game before downloading.
Record it on a real device (best quality, and Apple prefers real device footage). One ~20–25s clip
serves **both** stores.

## Specs

**Apple App Store "app preview":**
- Length **15–30 seconds** (hard limit — trim to this).
- Format `.mov` / `.mp4`, **H.264**. Portrait.
- Size: use the device's native portrait resolution — a **6.9" iPhone records at 1290×2796**, which is
  exactly the size App Store Connect wants for that device. (886×1920 / 1080×1920 are also accepted.)
- Up to 3 previews per device size. The **first frame is the poster** — end/lead on the goal moment.
- Uploaded in ASC → your version → Media, under the **6.9" iPhone** (and optionally **13" iPad**) slots.

**Google Play "promo video":**
- It's **a YouTube URL**, not an uploaded file. Upload the same clip to YouTube (unlisted or public),
  then paste the watch URL in Play Console → Main store listing → Video.

## How to record (iPhone, ~2 minutes)

1. Settings → Control Center → add **Screen Recording** to Control Center.
2. Open **Cap Kickers**. Pick a pitch/cap skin you like first (Settings) so it looks its best.
3. Swipe into Control Center, long-press the record button, **turn the mic OFF** (no room noise),
   tap Start, then return to the game.
4. Play the shot-list below (~25s). Stop recording from the status bar.
5. In **Photos**, trim the clip to **15–30s** (drag the yellow handles). Optionally polish in iMovie.
6. **Apple:** in App Store Connect, drag it into the 6.9" iPhone preview slot; set the poster frame to
   the goal celebration. **Play:** upload to YouTube, paste the link in the listing.

## Suggested ~25-second shot-list (one continuous take reads best)

| Time | Show |
|------|------|
| 0–3s | Home screen (the "Cap Kickers" title), tap **Practice** or **Campaign** |
| 3–10s | A real **flick** — cap slides with spin + trail; **thread it between** your other two caps |
| 10–18s | **Advance up the pitch** with 2–3 flicks; let the camera pan toward the goal |
| 18–24s | On the **5th touch the gate opens** — flick **past the keeper** → **GOAL** (confetti/pop) |
| 24–25s | Hold on the goal celebration (this becomes the poster frame) |

Tips: keep it one take (Apple likes genuine gameplay); miss-and-retry once is fine and shows the
rewarded "one more shot"; don't show the ad banner if you can help it (it only appears on menus).
