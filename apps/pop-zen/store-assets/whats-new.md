# Zen Bubbles — "What's New" (v1.2.0)

User-facing release notes for the **1.2.0** release (iOS `MARKETING_VERSION 1.2.0`
build 6 / Android `versionName 1.2.0` versionCode 2). This is a big feature update
over the shipped 1.0.x. Keep it warm and calm; **no version numbers in the copy and
no mention of ads.**

> Baseline note for whoever lists it: iOS players are coming from 1.0.x, so 1.2.0 is
> also their first time getting **haptics** and **Portuguese** (fold those into the
> iOS notes). The first Android release already shipped haptics + Portuguese, so the
> Android notes can focus on the new gameplay.

---

## App Store / Play — "What's New" (recommended)

```
Zen Bubbles just got a whole lot bigger:

• Two ways to play — Pop Challenge (beat the clock, chase goals) and Pop for Fun (relax, pop forever)
• Four "worlds" that keep it fresh — bubbles go off-grid, start drifting, then hide behind sliding shields
• A daily challenge with goals to come back for, plus a daily bonus and streaks
• Earn coins and spend them in the new Shop — collectible bubble styles and power-ups (Bomb, Time Freeze)
• Calm background music and a little celebration between rounds
• Smoother, snappier popping and lots of polish

Pop, relax, repeat. 🫧
```

**One-liner (if a short field is needed):**
```
New Shop, power-ups, a daily challenge, and four escalating worlds — plus calmer music and smoother popping.
```

## Portuguese (Brazil) — "Novidades"

O Zen Bubbles ficou muito maior:

• Quatro "mundos" que renovam o jogo — bolhas fora da linha, flutuantes e escondidas atrás de escudos
• Um desafio diário com metas, mais bônus diário e sequências
• Ganhe moedas e gaste na nova Loja — estilos de bolha e power-ups (Bomba, Congelar Tempo)

• Estouros mais suaves e muitos ajustes

Estoure, relaxe, repita. 🫧
