Zen Bubbles is a calm, satisfying bubble-popping game made for quick moments of
relaxation. No timers pressuring you, no clutter — just clean bubbles, a soft pop,
and a gentle challenge to beat your own best time.

Play through five soothing phases. The bubbles start big and get smaller as you go,
so each phase asks for a little more focus than the last. Pop them all as fast as you
can, then try to beat your record on the next run.

WHY YOU'LL LIKE IT
• Five calming phases, from Extra Large bubbles down to the tricky small ones
• A soft, realistic pop on every bubble — oddly satisfying
• Light haptic tap on each pop (toggle it on or off in Settings)
• Beat-your-best scoring that keeps every run fresh
• Calm, minimal design with a soothing color palette
• Works fully offline — no account, no sign-up, no fuss

BUILT TO RESPECT YOU
• No accounts and no personal sign-in
• Your best scores and settings stay on your device
• Free to play, supported by ads

Whether you have thirty seconds in a queue or a few quiet minutes before bed, Zen
Bubbles is an easy way to slow down, focus, and unwind. Pop a few. Feel better.
